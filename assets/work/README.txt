Verr Beauty: portfolio photos

Put her photos in this folder named 01.jpg to 12.jpg. Portrait (4:5) works best,
about 1000 px tall and under 200 KB each.

The site uses them automatically:
- 01, 04, 03 (in that order) are the three cards in the hero
- 01, 02, 03 are the photos on the Makeup, Lashes and Installations cards
- all 12 appear in "The work" gallery

Each number's caption and category (Makeup, Lashes, Installs) is set in the WORK list
near the bottom of index.html. Change a caption there if a photo shows a different look.
Any number without a photo shows a shade-swatch tile instead.
